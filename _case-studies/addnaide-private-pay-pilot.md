title: AddnAide Private Pay Pilot
description: >-
  Families paying for home care out of pocket had no way into AddnAide. In
  three weeks, discovery with the product owner became a signed scope, an
  estimate derived from the code, and 17 working screens that all agree with
  each other.
problem: >-
  AddnAide was built for program-funded care, where a care manager sets up the
  plan and a county program pays the caregiver. The pilot opened the door to
  families paying privately, who would sign themselves up, set up care, settle
  a rate with a caregiver, and get billed, with no care manager in the loop.
  The product owner needed a scope and an estimate she could defend, and the
  usual path, where a mockup is approved, a ticket is written from memory, and
  an estimate is written from the ticket, lets all three drift apart.
role: >-
  I led discovery for the pilot, running daily working sessions with
  AddnAide's product owner and turning each decision into story map cards,
  generated HTML wireframes, and an estimate read from the existing codebase.
  Then I built the demo screens in Phoenix LiveView and SCSS alongside the lead
  developer, with Claude agents I authored handling the overnight drafting and
  review.
outcome: >-
  The product owner left with a signed statement of work, a scope cut in the
  open from 128 mapped stories to 49 committed ones, and an estimate of about
  235 hours against a 240-hour implementation budget. The 17 working screens
  were built in five days and demoed on August 4, three weeks after kickoff.

solutions:
  - title: generated wireframes
    description: >-
      Every screen in the pilot started as an outline of what a person sees and
      does there, and the HTML wireframes were generated from those outlines.
      Each is tagged new, adapt, or existing and lists the story cards it
      belongs to, so when a decision moved, regenerating the set meant
      re-running it instead of redrawing it. The set went through nine
      revisions in eight days.
    media: /assets/images/case-studies/addnaide-private-pay-pilot-wireframe-index.png
  - title: setup checklist wireframe
    description: >-
      The wireframes carry their own reasoning. Notes above the employer
      dashboard spell out the rules its cards settled, like a checklist that
      only stays while something is outstanding, so the product owner, the
      developer, and the estimate all read the same decisions.
    media: /assets/images/case-studies/addnaide-private-pay-pilot-setup-checklist-wireframe.png
  - title: sign up
    description: >-
      A new visitor answers one question first: do they need care, or do they
      provide it? The fork sends families and caregivers to the right account
      from the very first screen.
    media: /assets/images/case-studies/addnaide-private-pay-pilot-sign-up.png
  - title: setup checklist
    description: >-
      Instead of a wizard that asks for everything up front, a new family lands
      on a checklist covering care needs, schedule, hourly rate, billing, and a
      few words about the person receiving care. Each item opens its own short
      form and can be done in any order.
    media: /assets/images/case-studies/addnaide-private-pay-pilot-setup-checklist.png
  - title: job offer
    description: >-
      Families and caregivers settle the rate between themselves. A caregiver
      sees the work and the rate offered, then accepts it, suggests another
      rate, or turns it down with a note, and her answer lands on the family's
      dashboard.
    media: /assets/images/case-studies/addnaide-private-pay-pilot-job-offer.png
  - title: visit timer
    description: >-
      Caregivers clock in and out from their dashboard. When a family is in
      more than one program, the caregiver picks which one the visit is for,
      and the monthly budget the hours draw from sits right under the timer.
    media: /assets/images/case-studies/addnaide-private-pay-pilot-visit-timer.png
  - title: invoices
    description: >-
      Private-pay families need something program clients never did, an
      invoice list. Each billing period shows its estimated amount and whether
      it's paid, pending, or overdue, with a plain note on where the numbers
      come from.
    media: /assets/images/case-studies/addnaide-private-pay-pilot-invoices.png
  - title: review progress
    description: >-
      A caregiver has to pass a program review and background checks before
      taking private-pay work. Her dashboard shows how far the review has come
      and the one thing she still owes, here a fingerprinting visit, so she is
      never guessing where she stands.
    media: /assets/images/case-studies/addnaide-private-pay-pilot-review-progress.png
